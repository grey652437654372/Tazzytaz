About Connell Auto Detail

Connell Auto Detail is a mobile car and truck detailing service based in Senoia, Georgia. We bring professional-level cleaning directly to your driveway, making it easy and affordable to keep your vehicle looking brand new.

We specialize in interior deep cleaning, exterior washes, and full details for daily drivers, trucks, and cars. Our goal is simple — clean, shine, and restore your vehicle without dealership pricing.
